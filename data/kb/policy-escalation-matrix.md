| Situation | Escalate to | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Self-harm or crisis signals | Team lead on duty + follow `sop-crisis-protocol` | Respond within 1 hour |
| Chargeback, bank dispute, lawyer or regulator mentioned | Senior billing | Same business day |
| Refund above $50 or outside policy | Team lead approval | 1 business day |
| Credit restore above 50 credits | Team lead approval | 1 business day |
| Advisor misconduct or suspected bot | Trust & Safety | 2 business days |
| Data deletion request | Privacy queue | 30 days (legal) |
| Bug affecting payments or many users | Tech support on call | Same day |
