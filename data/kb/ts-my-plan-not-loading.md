If a screen like "My Plan" stays blank or keeps loading:
1. Check your internet connection and switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
2. Log out and log back in.
3. Update the app.

To see when your subscription renews without the app: website purchases show it on nebula.example/account; App Store and Google Play purchases show it in the store's subscription settings.
