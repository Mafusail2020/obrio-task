Applies only to purchases made on the Nebula website (billed by Nebula, not by Apple or Google).

- **Duplicate charges** (same plan charged twice in one billing period): always refund the duplicate in full. No approval needed.
- **Charged after cancellation**: if the cancellation is visible in the admin panel before the charge date, refund in full. No approval needed.
- **Trial converted to a paid subscription**: full refund if the customer asks within 48 hours of the first paid charge AND has not used any paid feature since the conversion.
- **Accidental or unwanted purchase**: full refund within 14 days of the charge if the customer used at most 3 paid readings/reports in that period.
- **Anything else** (older than 14 days, heavy usage, partial months): no refund by default. The agent may offer bonus credits instead. Exceptions need team lead approval.
- Refunds above **$50** in total for one customer need team lead approval.

Refunds return to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Never promise a faster timeline.
