Advisors on Nebula must:
- Be respectful at all times; insults or mocking customers are a violation.
- Never ask for payment, gifts or contact details outside the app (PayPal, WhatsApp, email, "special rituals").
- Write their own replies. Fully automated or AI-generated replies presented as the advisor's own are a violation.

When a customer reports a violation:
1. Thank them and confirm the report is being reviewed by the Trust & Safety team.
2. Restore the credits spent on that session (see `policy-credits`).
3. Never share the outcome of an investigation or any details about the advisor's account.
4. If the customer already sent money outside the app, tell them Nebula cannot recover off-platform payments and advise them to contact their payment provider.
