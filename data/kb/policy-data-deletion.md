- Every deletion request must be completed within **30 days** (GDPR). Log the request date.
- Verify identity first: the request must come from the account email, or the customer must confirm via the verification link (see `sop-identity-verification`).
- Deletion is permanent: readings, chat history, credits and birth data are erased. Tell the customer this before confirming.
- Active website subscriptions must be cancelled as part of the deletion. App Store / Google Play subscriptions must be cancelled by the customer in the store; deletion does not stop store billing.
- Never ask the customer *why* they want deletion and never try to talk them out of it.
