1. Find the order in the admin panel (order IDs look like NB-12345).
2. Soulmate sketches and personal reports are delivered within **72 hours** of purchase to the app inbox and by email.
3. Under 72 hours: tell the customer the expected delivery time. Over 72 hours: click **Resend**; if the order shows "failed", trigger regeneration and tell the customer it will arrive within 24 hours.
4. If not delivered 24 hours after regeneration: full refund for that product (website purchase) or store refund instructions (store purchase).
