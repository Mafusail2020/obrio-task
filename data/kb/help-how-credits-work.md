Credits pay for chats with advisors; the advisor's rate per minute is shown before you start.
- Credit packs and bonus offers appear in your balance within a few minutes; bonus credits can take up to 24 hours.
- Credits do not expire.
- If something went wrong in a chat (the advisor didn't answer, or the app crashed), contact support and we will review the session.
