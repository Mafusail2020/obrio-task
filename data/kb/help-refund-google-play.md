Purchases made through Google Play are refunded by Google, not by Nebula.
1. Open **play.google.com** → **Order history** (or Google Play app → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history).
2. Find the Nebula purchase and select **Request a refund** / **Report a problem**.
3. Choose the reason and submit.
Within 48 hours of purchase Google often refunds automatically; after that, Google reviews the request.
