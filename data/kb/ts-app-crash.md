If the Nebula app crashes or freezes:
1. Update the app to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Restart your phone.
3. If it still crashes, delete and reinstall the app, then log in with the same method you used before (Apple, Google or email). Your purchases and credits are kept on your account.

Agents: if the steps don't help, collect device model, OS version, app version and what the customer was doing when it crashed, then file a bug. If credits were spent in a session that crashed, restore them (`policy-credits`).
