Use whenever a customer expresses hopelessness, wanting to die, self-harm, or not being able to go on, in any language.

1. **Respond personally within 1 hour.** Do not use templates, macros or AI drafts.
2. Lead with care, not with the account issue: acknowledge what they wrote, tell them they matter and that you are glad they reached out.
3. Share crisis resources: local emergency number if they are in immediate danger, and https://findahelpline.com to find a free, confidential helpline in their country.
4. **Do not** give readings, predictions or advice about their future, and do not suggest an advisor chat.
5. Resolve any billing question gently and generously (refund per policy without arguing), but only after the care message.
6. Notify the team lead on duty and add the "crisis" tag; the team lead checks in on the conversation.
