When a customer mentions a chargeback, bank dispute, lawyer or consumer authority:

1. Hand the ticket to senior billing the same business day (`policy-escalation-matrix`).
2. Do not argue, do not threaten account closure, and do not discourage the customer from contacting their bank.
3. Check the charge against `policy-refund-web`. If a refund is allowed, offer it clearly; a resolved refund usually prevents the dispute.
4. If a chargeback was already filed, do not refund separately (it would be a double refund); senior billing handles the dispute response.
