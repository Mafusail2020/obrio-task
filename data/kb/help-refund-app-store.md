Purchases made through the App Store are refunded by Apple, not by Nebula.
1. Go to **reportaproblem.apple.com** and sign in with your Apple ID.
2. Tap **I'd like to** → **Request a refund**.
3. Choose the reason and select the Nebula purchase.
Apple reviews the request and usually replies within 48 hours.
