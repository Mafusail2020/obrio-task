1. Open the chat session in the admin panel and copy the session ID.
2. Create a Trust & Safety report with: session ID, advisor name, what the customer reported, and quotes from the chat.
3. Restore the session credits (`policy-credits`).
4. Reply to the customer: the report is under review; we can't share the outcome. Do not speculate whether the advisor is a bot.
