If you subscribed on our website (for example after clicking an ad):
1. Go to **nebula.example/account** and log in with the email you used at checkout.
2. Open **Subscription** and click **Cancel auto-renewal**.
You will get a confirmation email. If you can't log in, reply to support from your purchase email and we will cancel it for you.
