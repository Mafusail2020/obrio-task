If your payment does not go through:
1. Check that the card details, expiry date and billing ZIP code are correct.
2. Some banks block international or in-app payments by default; ask your bank to allow payments to Nebula.
3. In the app, purchases go through your App Store or Google Play account, so make sure a valid payment method is set there.
4. Try another card or payment method.
We never see or store your full card number.
