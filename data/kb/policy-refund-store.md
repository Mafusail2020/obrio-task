Subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play are billed by Apple or Google, not by Nebula.

- Nebula **cannot** issue refunds for these purchases and cannot see the customer's card details.
- The customer must request the refund from the store (see help articles `help-refund-app-store` and `help-refund-google-play`). The store decides; agents must not promise an outcome.
- Cancellation must also be done in the store's subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
- If the customer is unsure where they paid: ask them to check their bank statement. "APPLE.COM/BILL" or "Google Play" means a store purchase; "NEBULA" or "OBRIO" means a website purchase.
