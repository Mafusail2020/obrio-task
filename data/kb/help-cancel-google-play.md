1. Open the **Google Play Store** app.
2. Tap your profile picture → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions**.
3. Select **Nebula** and tap **Cancel subscription**.
Your access stays active until the end of the current period. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.
