Credits are used to pay for chats with advisors. Credit restores work the same for every billing channel (website, App Store, Google Play), so there is no need to ask where the customer paid.

- Credits are **not refundable to a card**. Credit problems are resolved by restoring credits to the account.
- **Bonus credits** from an offer are added within 24 hours of purchase. If they are missing after 24 hours, the agent restores them.
- **Restore credits for a session** when: the advisor's average reply time was over 3 minutes, the advisor did not answer the question, or the session ended because of a technical failure.
- **Reading quality** complaints ("inaccurate", "generic"): one goodwill restore per customer per 30 days, up to the credits spent on that session.
- Agents can restore up to **50 credits** without approval. More than 50 credits needs team lead approval.
